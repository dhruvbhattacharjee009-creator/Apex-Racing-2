06 / Technical research & methodology

Research desk

A transparent research framework for understanding Formula One engineering, vehicle performance and race strategy.

AERODYNAMICS

Downforce and drag

Study the relationship between aerodynamic load, drag, ride height, airflow and vehicle balance.

  • Front-wing airflow management
  • Underfloor and diffuser performance
  • Rear-wing efficiency
  • Drag reduction and straight-line speed
VEHICLE DYNAMICS

Tyres, suspension and handling

Understand how tyre characteristics, mechanical grip and suspension influence cornering behaviour.

  • Tyre temperature and degradation
  • Load transfer and balance
  • Ride height and platform control
  • Corner entry, apex and exit
POWERTRAIN

Hybrid power systems

Explore how energy recovery, deployment and thermal management affect lap performance.

  • Energy recovery systems
  • Electrical power deployment
  • Combustion engine efficiency
  • Cooling and thermal constraints
RACE STRATEGY

Race engineering

Analyse strategic decisions and how teams respond to changing race conditions.

  • Pit-stop windows
  • Tyre strategy
  • Safety-car scenarios
  • Undercut and overcut concepts
